Denoising, step by step

A diffusion language model starts from a block of masks and fills in a few positions per step, in whatever order it is most confident about. Each replay below is a real answer from our evaluation runs, with every token placed at the step the model committed it. Press play, drag the slider, or switch on order to colour each token by when it was written.

Maths: who writes left to right?

The converted model fills positions almost strictly in reading order, like an autoregressive model. LLaDA, trained as diffusion from scratch, jumps around. With 32 steps instead of 256, the conversion must commit 8 tokens per step, and the answer falls apart.

Infilling: the gap has to be the right size

The code above and below the gap is fixed; only the masked gap is written. Told the exact gap length, the conversion fills it perfectly. Given 1.5 times the room, it writes the right line, then starts the next line inside the gap and breaks the program. LLaDA spends its spare positions on a comment.

Reversal: taught one way, asked the other

Our Qwen conversion was taught 1,000 invented facts, in the direction name to role only, for 10,000 updates. It answers 999 of them forward and none in reverse; on every reverse question it repeats the question back.
